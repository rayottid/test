8 · Key Findings & Data Gaps

Summary for Decision Makers
🔍 Key Findings
📈
Feimei brands dominate at Sociolla — Skintific alone records IDR 420M at Sociolla Mantos, dwarfing all L'Oréal and Paragon brands combined at that store. Timephoria is growing at both surveyed stores.
📉
Maybelline & Wardah declining universally — Both brands show downward trends at both Sociolla and CuraBeauty. L'Oréal Paris is declining at CuraBeauty while stable at Sociolla.
💡
Skincare category expanding; Personal Care shrinking — Both stores confirmed Skincare is gaining shelf space and prominence. Personal Care is being deprioritised at both locations.
🧪
Skintific & Hada Labo dominate skincare purchases — Across all 6 skincare customer interviews, Skintific and Hada Labo appeared most frequently in final purchases, driven by niacinamide brightening formulas and loyalty to the brand.
🎯
TikTok & KOL content driving purchase decisions — Multiple category BA reports cite "TikTok promotion," "KOL/Seleb content," and "influencer" as key drivers of top-selling products (OMG, BNB, Implora, G2G, Timephoria).
📦
Bundle strategy largely absent — Across all 14 product categories surveyed, the dominant response to "top bundles" is "Tidak ada" (none) or "No Bundle." Only isolated examples exist (e.g., OMG 2-shade bundle, DM Buy 2 Get 1 Free).
⚠️ Data Gaps & Inconsistencies
🔲
Stores 3–6 completely empty — The survey template allocated rows for 6 stores per channel; only Stores 1 and 2 have any data. Remaining 4 store slots have no respondents recorded.
💰
Sociolla revenue data missing entirely — Store 1 (Sociolla Mantos) recorded "Not Provide" for all product revenue, share %, and ATV data across every makeup and skincare category. This limits benchmarking between the two stores.
🏷️
Garnier size = 0 at Sociolla; several brands listed with "0" — Brand size fields for Garnier (Sociolla), OMG, Emina, Crystallure, lite+, Tavi (Sociolla) are recorded as numeric "0" in the source — likely indicating no presence, not actual zero revenue.
📋
Price data mostly inaccessible — For all brands, price adjustment data is answered as "tidak dapat memberikan data" (cannot provide data). Only Maybelline confirmed some price increases without specifics.
👥
Customer interview slots 4–6 (Makeup & Skincare) unfilled — The template allowed for 6 customer observations per category but only 3 per category have recorded data. Customer slots 4, 5, 6 are blank.
🏪
KA channel master (Sheet7) shows all accounts as "待分工 / To assign" — Guardian, Sociolla, Watsons and other KA accounts in the channel master table have no data-availability level or qualified store marked yet.
